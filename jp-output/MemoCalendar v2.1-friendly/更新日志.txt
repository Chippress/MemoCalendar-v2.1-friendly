MemoCalendar Changelog
Author: Chippress

v2.1-friendly (2026-10-03)
- Packaged with jpackage (Java 25) to avoid the "No JVM could be found" issue.

v2.1.0 (2026-08-31)
- Rebuilt source code, improving the logic of opening and closing the windows and dialogs.
- Added support for creating and choosing different calendar documents within this app.
- Almost no AIGC.

v2.0.0 (2026-08-28)
- Completely rebuilt based on AWT/Swing (Java 11).
- Added support for direct information editing by clicking on a date.
- Almost no AIGC.
- Only breads for some meals while developing.

v0.1.x (2026-01-23)
- Experimental release.
- Developed with EasyX (C++14).
- Implemented basic anniversary calendar features.
- Contains minimal AIGC.
